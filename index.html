About

Huddle is a simple way to sit down with friends over video, whether you're studying together or just talking.

How it works

Start a room and share the link. Whoever opens it joins your call. There are no accounts and nothing to install.

Calls travel directly between your browsers. When a network won't allow that, an encrypted relay carries the call instead.

What stays private

  • Your mic and camera are off when you join. Nothing is captured until you switch them on.
  • Calls aren't recorded and chat isn't saved. When the call ends, it's gone.
  • Files you share go to the people in the room and aren't stored on any server.
  • Audio and video are encrypted between browsers. The services that help calls connect can't see or hear them.
  • Your name and settings are remembered in your own browser, not on a server.

In a call

  • Screen sharing tuned for sharp text
  • Chat with files and images
  • A host who can mute people and manage the room
  • Mute or hide anyone just for yourself
  • Quality settings for slow connections
  • A noise filter for fans, traffic and typing
  • The live delay to each person, shown on their tile

Good to know

Huddle works best with up to about six people. Everyone connects to everyone, so larger rooms get heavy.

Huddle is a personal project. You can read its code on GitHub.
